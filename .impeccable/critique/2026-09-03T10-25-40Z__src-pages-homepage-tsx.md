# Homepage Critique - 2026-09-03

Target: src/pages/HomePage.tsx
URL: http://localhost:5173/
Method: in-thread design review plus Impeccable detector. Sub-agent critique was attempted but unavailable because the agent thread limit was reached.

Score: 28/32
Detector: pass, no findings.

What works:
- The homepage now feels much closer to Nokta's premium discovery direction: clean hero, strong type, warm background, dark footer CTA, and restrained cards.
- The search entry is clear and useful, with sensible quick filters.
- Category cards are strong as an above-the-fold bridge from brand promise to browsing.
- The page has enough content depth for launch: categories, featured venues, offers, popular venues, areas, cities, how it works, testimonials, CTA.

Main issues:
1. The page is slightly too long and repetitive for a homepage. Category cards, featured venues, offers, book-worthy venues, browse by area, city cards, how it works and testimonials are all valid, but together they dilute the sharpness of the landing experience.
2. The hero copy is clear but a little generic. "Discover social venues worth going out for" is polished, but it could carry more of Nokta's specific point of view: finding the right spot, not just browsing venues.
3. The quick filter chips still feel functional rather than premium. They are useful, but visually they read closer to filters than editorial prompts.
4. The category cards and later area/city cards share similar card mechanics, so the homepage risks feeling card-heavy even after the old banner was removed.
5. Testimonials are useful later, but they currently feel placeholder-like. On a venue discovery product, real usage signals or editorial confidence would likely feel stronger than generic quotes.

Top polish priorities:
1. Tighten the page hierarchy: hero, category cards, featured/offer content, one browsing section, CTA. Consider removing either "How it works" or testimonials for launch.
2. Make the hero more Nokta-specific: use language around choosing the right spot, plans, neighbourhoods, and confidence before booking.
3. Restyle quick filters as editorial shortcuts, for example "Open tonight", "Good for groups", "Outdoor spots", "Under GBP20", instead of plain utility filters.
4. Reduce card sameness by making city/area browsing more compact, closer to a link list or editorial index.
5. Replace generic testimonials with proof once available: number of venues, cities covered, owner-managed/claimed venues, or recent updates.

Heuristic notes:
- Clarity: 8/8. The page tells users what to do and routes them properly.
- Visual hierarchy: 7/8. Strong hero and CTA, but mid-page sections compete.
- Consistency: 7/8. Components fit the current Nokta system, though cards are repeated heavily.
- Launch readiness: 6/8. Solid base, but the homepage still needs sharper editorial prioritisation.
