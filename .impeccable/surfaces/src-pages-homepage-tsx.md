## Scope

Homepage `/` behind `mapHome` flag. Mode: Persuade (lands visitor on a place fast). Mobile first.

## Audience & job

Someone out or about to go out who wants somewhere near, open, with honest price and closing time.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The place is the brand: venues are dots on a quiet custom map. Refuses the hero-plus-search-plus-rails booking homepage and the busy Google-tile map.
OWN-WORLD: Cream map ground, faint ink streets, uppercase neighbourhood labels, ink dots for open, muted for closed, one clay dot for the selected venue. Paper bottom sheet, ink chips, Outfit.
STORY: Visitor sees what's open around them, taps a dot, reads price and closing time, opens the venue.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Map fills screen under the site header. Search pill + city at top. Locate button right. Bottom sheet at ~42% height with count heading, filter chips, swipeable venue cards. Selected dot shows ink pill: name · £price · till time.
FORM: Dot map, rank 7 on structure list, seed aeb56216.
