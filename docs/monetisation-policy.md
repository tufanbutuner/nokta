# Sheesha Monetisation Policy

This is internal guidance for future commercial work.

- Sponsored placements must be clearly labelled as Featured, Sponsored or Promoted.
- Paid status must never hide poor data quality, unsafe venue status or missing verification.
- Do not directly promote tobacco products, tobacco flavours, smoking claims, tobacco giveaways or tobacco coupons.
- Keep consumer trust higher than short-term monetisation.
- Keep editorial lists separate from sponsored placements.
- Always distinguish organic ranking from paid placement.
