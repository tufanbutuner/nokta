# Nokta owner analytics value reporting

## Purpose

Owner analytics should answer one practical question: is Nokta helping this venue get discovered, trusted and booked?

## Owner metrics

- Profile views: `venue_profile_viewed`.
- Booking CTA clicks: `venue_booking_cta_clicked`.
- Booking requests: `venue_booking_request_submitted`.
- Enquiries: `venue_enquiry_submitted`.
- Customer actions: directions, website, Instagram and save events.
- Promoted offers: `promoted_offer_viewed` and `promoted_offer_clicked`.
- Featured placements: `featured_placement_viewed` and `featured_placement_clicked`.

## Date ranges

Owner analytics supports last 7 days, last 30 days, this month, last month and all time. The default is last 30 days.

## Plan access

Free claimed venues see basic summary counts. Starter and above can see daily trends. Growth and above can see conversion funnel reporting. Pro can see promoted offer and featured placement reporting.

## Profile completeness

The profile completeness card scores description, opening hours, photos, address, contact links, booking readiness, availability and category/vibe detail.

## Privacy

Analytics pages show aggregate counts only. They must not expose customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, raw booking tokens, enquiry text or exact customer location. Customer details remain available only inside legitimate booking and enquiry management workflows.
