# Owner Enquiry Inbox

Sprint 29 adds the Growth+ owner enquiry inbox.

## Access

- Owner enquiry inbox is available on Growth and Pro.
- Owners can only access enquiries for claimed venues they own.
- The subscription must be trial, active or past due.
- Free and Starter owners can see enquiry summary counts only.
- Free and Starter owners see an upgrade prompt instead of customer details.

## Owner Workflow

Owners can view customer contact details, enquiry message, preferred date/time, party size and enquiry type for accessible venues.

Owners can update enquiry status to contacted, responded, converted, closed or spam. They can also add venue response and owner notes.

Owner notes are visible to the venue account and Sheesha admins. Admin notes remain admin-only.

## Admin Oversight

Admins retain full enquiry oversight and controls. The admin enquiry dashboard shows owner handling indicators, owner notes and venue response.

## Out Of Scope

Enquiries are not confirmed bookings. This sprint does not add in-app messaging, email notifications, payment/deposit handling, instant booking or customer replies inside Sheesha.

## Analytics

Owner enquiry analytics do not track customer names, emails, phone numbers, enquiry messages, owner notes or venue response text.
