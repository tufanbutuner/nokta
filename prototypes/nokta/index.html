The dot is the brand

The earlier drafts could have been any directory site. These are built from the atom Nokta already owns — the dot — and what it means for a product about places. Three concepts, one philosophy.

A dot is a placeEvery venue is a point with a coordinate, not a card in a grid.
A dot is a decisionNokta is the full stop — the end of the search, "this is the one".
Dots connectA saved shortlist is a constellation; a plan is the shape you draw together.
Dots are livePins pulse and dim with real hours — the map is alive, not a directory.
